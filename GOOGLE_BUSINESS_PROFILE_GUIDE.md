## Review Management

### Review Collection Strategy

#### 1. Create Direct Review Link
Your Google review link format:
```
https://g.page/r/[YOUR_BUSINESS_ID]/review
```

To find your review link:
1. Open your Google Business Profile
2. Click "Home" in menu
3. Click "Get more reviews"
4. Copy and share the short URL

#### 2. Email Template for Review Requests
```
Subject: How was your locksmith service?

Would you mind taking 60 seconds to share your experience?

[Leave a Google Review Button/Link]

Thank you for your business!

Best regards,
A Secure Annapolis Locksmith Team
(410) 849-6069
```
